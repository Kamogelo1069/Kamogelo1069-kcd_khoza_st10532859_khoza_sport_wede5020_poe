## Brief History 
Khoza Sport is a company founded in 2018 by ex-athletic trainer Kamo Khoza in Johannesburg, South Africa. Initially, the business was launched as a retail shop selling sporting goods, including soccer boots and clothing. Over the past 8 years, the company has expanded its sales network across Johannesburg, supplying local sports teams with equipment. The company registered significant success with its products and began to sell its merchandise in local sports centers. Khoza sport now intends to take advantage of the high demand for its products and launch an online platform to sell its sporting equipment. 

## Website Goals and Objectives
•	To create an official website to display the organization’s products, their prices and receive direct inquiries and sales from South African customers
## Specific Goals 
 •	Set up an online form system to take orders and large quotations from schools and local sports clubs
•	Lastly, showcase the brand's reliability and trustworthiness by providing full product specifications and sizes online alongside reviews.
## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) 
•	KPI 1: 35 average sales/inquiry form submissions per month (on a 3-month period commencing launch) – achieved.
•	KPI 2: 1,500 average monthly overall traffic (organic unique visitors) is the target to be delivered within 6 months from launch of Sales and Marketing website.
•	KPI 3: Keeping average mobile site page load speed under 3,0 seconds to avoid high bounce rate.
